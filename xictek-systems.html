About the Company

Who We Are

XICTEK Systems is a Pakistani software company focused on building practical, accessible technology products. We design and develop software that solves real problems for businesses, entrepreneurs and individuals — starting with the markets and users we know best.

Our flagship product, HisabDo, is an offline-first digital ledger and khata management app used by shopkeepers, freelancers and small businesses across Pakistan and beyond.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Build software that works for real people. We build tools that fit the way people already work — not the other way around. Every product we create starts with a real problem observed in the field, not a feature list on a whiteboard.

Our Vision

Practical technology for every business. We envision a future where every small business, freelancer and entrepreneur has access to reliable, affordable and easy-to-use software — regardless of internet connectivity or technical background.

Why Businesses Choose XICTEK Systems
